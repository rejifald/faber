# Design drift, measured 2026-10-18

A snapshot taken before any system-level design decision. It is the context for the
design-system ADR. It is not kept current: re-measure rather than edit it.

The three surfaces are listed in [guidelines.md](guidelines.md). "Web" is the dashboard in
`src/web.rs`. The sign-in page (`src/control.rs`) and the standby page (`src/standby.rs`)
are small, so their numbers are given separately where they matter.

## Summary

- **The popover and the panel have one palette, copied by hand under two naming schemes.**
  19 of the 21 tokens have identical values in both themes. The two that differ are the
  shadow, which differs on purpose because the popover floats, and `0.1` written as `0.10`.
- **The web dashboard has no tokens.** It uses 20 distinct raw colours across 74
  declarations, in a GitHub-dark palette, dark only, set entirely in monospace. 11 of those
  colours are within ΔE 5 (OKLab ×100) of a panel dark token. The ones that are not are a
  blue link colour (the panel's accent is amber) and tinted status fills.
- **There is no scale for spacing, size, radius or type.** Spacing uses 25, 20 and 12
  distinct px values on the three surfaces; font sizes include half pixels (10.5, 11.5,
  12.5).
- **Controls size themselves.** The same "button" has three paddings across the surfaces,
  and the popover has four button shapes.

## Method

The figures come from the stylesheets at `main` 043c0b1, with comments stripped. "In
rules" means outside custom-property declarations. ΔE is the Euclidean OKLab distance
×100, measured against the panel's dark theme, because the web dashboard is dark only.
Controls are the rules whose selector names a button, chip, pill, input or menu item, with
hover and focus states left out.
